Get to Know Us

A Ministry Called to Transform Lives

Remnant of Lord Ministries is a Christian ministry founded on November 25, 2010, by Evangelist Elvis Sondera, following a divine calling and vision received from God.

The ministry was established in obedience to God's calling, with a mission to transform people into disciples of Jesus Christ and raise a remnant of believers committed to following Christ and spreading the Gospel.

We believe that the Gospel must reach every community, every generation, and every sphere of society.

  • Sharing the message of salvation.
  • Strengthening believers through the Word.
  • Promoting prayer and spiritual growth.
  • Making disciples of Jesus Christ.
